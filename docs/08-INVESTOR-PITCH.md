# 08 — Investor Pitch (Seibel framework, filled with SAKSI's numbers)

Built on Y Combinator / Michael Seibel's seed-pitch guidance. Two rules that shape
everything: **clear and consistent beats sexy**, and **the investor pitch is a
different pitch from the customer pitch** (investors want "can you build something
big"; customers want "does it solve my problem"). Elements below in Seibel's order;
middle sections ordered most-interesting-first. Show the *math*, and *actually ask*.

## 1. What we do (2 sentences + a specific example, jargon-free)

> "SAKSI is how Indonesia's social-media sellers prove they're trustworthy. A
> seller sends buyers a payment link instead of a bare bank number; the buyer pays
> and uploads the receipt; and every completed deal builds the seller a track
> record they can't fake — free, and it can't be bought. Sellers pay Rp20k once for
> a saved account and a track-record badge, or Rp200k/year for a branded storefront;
> buyers can pay Rp30k to add a legally-stamped e-meterai to a completed deal — and
> because we quietly see each seller's real income, we can later connect proven
> sellers to lenders for a fee."

Example (use the specific story): *Sinta runs a jastip. She wants Rp10M to fund a
big order but no bank will touch her — no salary slip, no records. SAKSI has watched
her earn ~Rp15M/month for 8 months with almost no disputes, so a lender says yes and
we take a cut.*

## 2. Team

[Founder to fill: who's building it, why they can build it, and — Seibel's
favorite — how they personally experienced the problem. State plainly any concrete
credential; don't bury it. No life story.]

## 3. Traction — **currently the weak slide; be honest**

Pre-launch. Do NOT fake traction (Seibel: fake/thin traction is worse than none).
The honest asset today is the **original 64-case research + a working reframed app**.
The number to go get before pitching (always **with time**): *"In N months, solo, X
sellers sent Saksi links and Y transactions were witnessed — with zero marketing
spend."* The single tell-tale metric is the **2nd-link rate** (does a seller send a
second invoice?) — that's the whole company in one number.

## 4. Unique insight — **the strongest slide, all numbers**

> "We ran an original 64-case study of Indonesian scam victims. Two findings: **0 of
> 42** checked anything before paying, and **0 of 11** who reported to institutions
> ever got their money back. Verification tools already exist — Kredibel,
> cekrekening — and **not one victim used them.** The insight: you cannot make
> trust-first buyers 'check.' But sellers already send payment details the second a
> buyer says 'kak aku mau bayar' — so we don't add a step, we **substitute the
> link.** And we key trust to the QRIS merchant ID, which stays constant even when
> scammers rename themselves — we watched one do it across **4 aliases in 13
> months.**"

## 5. Market size — **show the math, don't quote reports**

- Bottoms-up: **~5M** off-marketplace social sellers (SAM).
- Consumer revenue: Rp20k one-time (Akun Saksi) / Rp200k-per-year (Toko Saksi
  Pro) seller tiers + Rp30k buyer-initiated stamped receipts (Saksi Resmi).
- The real market is **credit**: Indonesia's MSME credit gap is **$165–234B**;
  digital lending is **$15B, +29%/yr**.
- The calculation: *"If just 100,000 of our sellers borrow Rp15M twice a year,
  that's **Rp30 trillion** in loan volume; at a 2.5% referral fee, **~Rp75B/year
  (~$4M)** from lending alone — 2% of our SAM against a $165B gap."*
- Comparable: OJK-licensed ICS providers (e.g. TrustDecision) already monetize
  alternative data; SAKSI owns the one dataset they can't get — *verified*
  off-platform income.

## 6. The ask — **amount + an 18–24 month milestone**

Lead with capital efficiency (a strength investors reward):

> "We reach break-even on **~$800** — this is bootstrappable. We're raising
> **$[50–150k]** to skip two years: hire our second person, grow to **10,000
> sellers**, and launch the credit-data product — hitting **~Rp30M/month revenue and
> our first lending pilot within 24 months.**"

The milestone (10,000 sellers + credit product live + lending pilot) *is* the ask —
not who else is investing.

## 7. Delivery notes (Seibel)

- Front-load the good stuff (investors decide in the first 2 minutes).
- Boring, basic slides — you want them looking at you, not a designer's work.
- Let the investor talk; the more they talk themselves into it, the more likely they
  invest.
- **Keep two pitches:** the above is the *investor* pitch. The *customer* pitch to a
  seller is different and benefit-first: *"Jualan tepercaya, pakai Saksi — kirim
  link, bukan nomor rekening."* (Note: for customer-facing copy inside the app the
  invariant still forbids "aman"/"terpercaya" as a guarantee; the pitch line above
  is external marketing framing, not an in-app string — keep the distinction.)

## 8. The honest read for the pitch
- **Strongest:** the unique insight (original research, hard numbers) + the market
  math (bottoms-up, credit-gap-anchored). Most founders don't have these.
- **Weakest: traction** — no projection fixes it; go get one real "we did X in N
  months" number first.
- The asymmetry that is the whole case: **downside is ~$800 and a year; upside is a
  credit-data business on a $165B gap.**
